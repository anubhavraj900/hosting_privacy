Privacy Policy

Kanpur Metro Guide (com.anubhav.raj.kanpurmetro)

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Kanpur Metro Guide ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Anubhav Rajput as an independent offline transit information guide for commuters using the Uttar Pradesh Metro Rail Corporation (UPMRC) Kanpur Metro network. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled.

1. Information Collection and Use

Kanpur Metro Guide operates primarily as an offline application. We do not require account registration, login credentials, or personal identity details to use any features in the app.

2. Location Data (GPS)

The app requests access to your device's location (Fine and Coarse GPS location) for the sole purpose of providing real-time transit utilities:

  • Detecting your nearest Kanpur Metro station on the home screen.
  • Sorting nearby metro stations by distance from your current position.

Important: Your location data is processed 100% locally on your device. Location data is never transmitted, uploaded, stored, or shared with any external servers, third parties, or analytics providers.

3. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes standard Android operating system services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services Location API: Used exclusively on-device to fetch hardware GPS coordinates.
  • Google Maps: Launched externally via Android Intent when you tap "Navigation" to view driving directions.

4. Data Security

Because the app does not store or transmit personal user data to cloud databases, your data remains completely private on your personal mobile device. All payment interactions (such as optional voluntary developer coffee donations) occur directly inside certified, official UPI banking applications (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with bank-level 256-bit encryption.

5. Children's Privacy

Kanpur Metro Guide does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is safe for public transit commuters of all ages.

6. Official Disclaimer

Kanpur Metro Guide is an independent, community-focused transit guide developed for public convenience. This application is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by the Uttar Pradesh Metro Rail Corporation (UPMRC) or Kanpur Metro Rail Corporation.
